«اريد لكل منطقة مجسمات مباني مختلفة حسب المكان اذا كان مناطق بالشرق مبانيها تبدو سكنية عادية او عائلية كلما اتجهت غربا كلما اصبحت مناطق مدنية وعمرانية ومباني عالية وسياحية».

«سوف ازودك بصور لمباني معمرة ومباني مدمرة او اماكن مدمرة ومن هذه الصور تستوحي كيف تصمم المجسمات».

**Why:** The user asked for location-specific building designs inspired by supplied images, rather than one repeated building style across the city.

**How to apply:** Use the user's references when designing buildings and destruction. Keep eastern areas residential/family-oriented and progressively introduce taller, more urban and tourist-oriented buildings toward the west.

## Destruction must read differently from unfinished construction

An exposed concrete frame alone is not enough. Preserve obvious structural failure in the exported silhouette: fractured and tilted slabs, breached irregular walls, broken columns and substantial mixed rubble. Regional character must remain recognizable through that damage.

**Why:** The first FBX proof renders had clean floor plates and sparse debris, making the models look like buildings under construction rather than the collapsed buildings in the references.

**How to apply:** Inspect renders of the actual exported FBX, not only the authoring scene or triangle counts. Allocate the polygon budget so collapsed slabs and ground rubble survive simplification; reduce repetitive clean-frame detail first.
