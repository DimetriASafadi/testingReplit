---
name: Neighborhood overview
description: The user's zoom-out target and how to judge it across phone orientations.
---

At maximum zoom-out, the user wants to see roughly 85% or more of the playable neighborhood on the screen, in both portrait and landscape.

**Why:** A larger numeric zoom range alone does not meet the request. The surrounding decorative terrain is much larger than the playable district, and phone aspect ratios and interface overlays change how much of the actual work area is visible.

**How to apply:** Judge the visible playable plots, not the decorative terrain border. Check the centered overview on real viewport proportions with the HUD and navigation present, and verify that the wide view can be saved and restored. Preserve the ability to zoom back in normally.
