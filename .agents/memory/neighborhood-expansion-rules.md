## Product direction requested 2026-10-12
Each building's entire parcel should be professionally landscaped for its purpose, with integrated paved edges, appropriate parking/trees/service space, consistent Gaza identity and coherent four-face artwork. Peripheral displacement tents should fade gradually as district completion rises.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to use the empty space around buildings rather than leave bare squares, and linked the disappearance of tents to reconstruction progress.

**How to apply:** Treat the surroundings as part of each building's art, preserving functional entrances and street access. This is requested direction, not a statement that all art has been implemented.

District completion must not reach 100% while any buildable parcel remains empty or unfinished. The requested street system lets players pave individual roughly parcel-length segments using a paving machine departing from an asphalt factory. Completion triggers fireworks; claiming moves directly into the next district.

**Why:** The user wants full reconstruction, not merely rubble removal, to define completion.

**How to apply:** Keep progress and claim eligibility consistent, preserve previously earned district access, and never re-award already claimed completion rewards.

The requested school, hospital, mosque and church have no income; sports club and recreational park do earn income. Easy/medium/hard should reduce income and moderately increase work times without requiring purchases to progress; final numerical tuning is deferred.

**Why:** These are explicit additions and income exceptions to the existing economic rules.

**How to apply:** Configure values centrally, preserve ongoing saved deadlines, and do not assume provisional balance numbers are final user-approved settings.

Difficulty reduces the profit of repeatable investments, not their returned principal. An already-earned building payout keeps its old value until collected; rebalance its next cycle instead.

**Why:** Scaling the full investment payout can turn the hard-mode farming loop into a loss, blocking progression; changing an earned payout also recreates the user's displayed-versus-collected income complaint.

**How to apply:** Maintain these guarantees when the user later supplies final difficulty ratios or investment prices.

The requested wheel has a two-hour free cooldown and rare large prizes. Real-money purchasing and rewarded-ad repeats are preparation only until providers are connected; gem-to-coin exchange should actually work.

**Why:** The user explicitly postponed payment and advertising connections while requesting their interfaces now.

**How to apply:** Clearly disable unconnected external purchase/ad options; never simulate payment success or watched ads. Before commercial activation, add authoritative verification rather than trusting local saves.

The supplied wheel artwork guides its appearance, but the user chose to retain the existing 150-coin reward while adding iron and wood, rather than copying the reference's reward list exactly.

**Why:** The explicit choice was “إضافة الحديد والخشب مع إبقاء 150 عملة”; the reference has fewer sectors than the accepted game wheel.

**How to apply:** Do not remove the retained coin prize when refining the reference-based art. Preserve the meaning of saved reward indices when adding further prizes.
